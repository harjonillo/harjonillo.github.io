1. Boil the mung beans until soft. This takes about 40 mins. Drain water.
2. Lightly stir-fry the garlic, onion in the olive oil 
on low-medium heat. Lower the heat to the lowest possible, and add the white miso.
3. Add back the cooked mung beans into the aromatics. Add additional water and 
olive oil as desired (I prefer a creamier consistency, almost like hummus).
Once it has reached the desired consistency, add in the alugbati and allow it to 
simmer on very low heat for another 5 minutes. Salt to taste.
4. Serve with rice.


**Note**: Ingredient weights to be verified

**Extra**: Bake it like gratin! Layer this over rice on an oven-friendly dish 
and drizzle a bit of olive oil. It gets brown and crusty without cheese or breadcrumbs.
